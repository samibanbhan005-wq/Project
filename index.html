Questions

Frequently asked questions

How soon is my plan activated after depositing?

Deposits are reviewed manually. In most cases verification completes within 30 minutes, after which your plan activates automatically and the first daily profit is credited within 24 hours.

Which payment methods does Veltra support?

Deposit and payout wallet details are shown inside your dashboard once you sign in. Withdrawals can be requested to your preferred local wallet or bank account.

How do referral commissions work?

You earn 10% of your referral's first deposit instantly, and afterwards 1.5% of the daily profit their active plan generates. Commissions are credited straight to your dashboard balance.

What happens if my withdrawal is rejected?

Rejected requests are refunded to your balance automatically — no amount is lost. You'll see the refund entry in your transaction history.
