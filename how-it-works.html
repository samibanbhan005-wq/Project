How It Works

From registration to payout — fully explained

Veltra keeps every step visible from your dashboard, so you always know where your money is.

1. Create your account

Register with your first name, last name, username, phone number, email and password. No OTP verification is required, so your account is live immediately. If a friend invited you, paste their referral code during sign-up to lock in their commission tracking.

2. Choose an investment tier

Pick Beginner (Rs 1,200), Intermediate (Rs 1,600), Advanced (Rs 2,600) or VIP Master (Rs 4,000). Each tier has its own daily profit rate and cycle length.

3. Deposit via SadaPay

Transfer the exact plan amount to the official Veltra wallet (Tanzeel Ul Rahman · 03289693983). Then open the deposit page and submit your proof form with your sender number and Transaction ID (TID).

4. Verification & activation

Your deposit appears as Pending until the admin matches your TID against the wallet statement. On approval your plan activates and, if this was your first deposit, your referrer receives their 10% instant bonus.

5. Daily profit crediting

Every 24 hours your profit is added to your dashboard balance automatically and logged in your transaction history. Your referrer also receives 1.5% of that profit as a recurring commission.

6. Withdraw your earnings

Request a payout to SadaPay, EasyPaisa or JazzCash. The amount is held from your balance immediately; once approved it is dispatched, and if rejected it is refunded to your balance automatically.

FAQ

Everything you might ask

Do I need OTP verification to sign up?

No. Veltra registration requires only your name, username, phone, email and password. Your account is created instantly and you can log in right away.

How do I log in?

Use either your username or your email address with your password on the login page.

Where do I send my deposit?

All deposits go to the official SadaPay wallet — Title: Tanzeel Ul Rahman, Number: 03289693983, IBAN: PK50 SADA 0000 0032 8969 3983. Always confirm the details on the deposit page inside your account.

How long does deposit approval take?

Most deposits are verified within 30 minutes during support hours. You can track the status on your dashboard as Pending, Approved or Rejected.

What is a TID and where do I find it?

The Transaction ID (TID) is the reference SadaPay generates for your transfer. It appears on your payment confirmation screen and in your transaction history.

When is my daily profit credited?

Profit accrues every 24 hours from the moment your plan is activated and is added to your balance automatically for the entire cycle length of the tier.

Can I have more than one active plan?

Your account holds one active plan at a time. Once a cycle completes you can activate a new tier by submitting a fresh deposit.

How much can I withdraw and how fast?

Minimum Rs 500 per request with no fixed upper limit other than your available balance. Approved withdrawals are dispatched within 24 hours to your chosen wallet.

What happens if my withdrawal is rejected?

The held amount is refunded to your Veltra balance instantly and a refund entry appears in your transaction history. Nothing is deducted for rejected requests.

Is there any joining or monthly fee?

No. Veltra charges no registration fee and no monthly maintenance fee. You only fund the investment tier you choose.
