Deposit steps

1. Select your plan(s)
Choose one or more tiers. Several plans can run at the same time and the exact total is shown below.
2. Send the exact amount
Transfer to the wallet using your wallet or banking app.
3. Submit the proof form
Enter your sender number and the Transaction ID (TID) from your receipt.
4. Wait for activation
Status stays Pending until admin approval — usually within 30 minutes.
