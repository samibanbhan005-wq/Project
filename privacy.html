Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Veltra collects, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you register we collect your name, username, email address and mobile number. We also record the deposit proofs, withdrawal requests and transaction history associated with your account.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to operate your account, verify deposits, process withdrawals, calculate referral commissions, provide support and meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be shared with service providers who help us run the platform, or with authorities where we are legally required to do so.

4. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and transaction records for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to satisfy legal, accounting and reporting requirements.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, subject to our legal obligations to retain certain records.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to cache data for faster loading. This information stays on your device and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

8. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at support@veltra.pk.
